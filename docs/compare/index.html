How these were made

Frames are single stills from UHD Blu-ray HDR10 films (BT.2020, PQ). Tesseract VP renders each one at its default settings for a 100-nit projector, measuring the frame itself as the Cube does. The other DTM solution's pictures were captured from its own output at its 100-nit setting, on the same frames.

Both pictures are the BT.2020 signal sent to the projector, shown as it is with no colour conversion, so on an ordinary screen colours look a little less saturated than on a wide-gamut projector, equally for both. The numbers are measured on the pictures as sent to the projector, black bars left out. Highlight detail held at full counts pixels where a colour channel stays at full (253 of 255 or more) across the pixel and its neighbours while the HDR frame still varies there by more than 4%; below 0.3% of the picture it reads none.
