HDMI link

Signalled to the display

PQ check: grey bars every 100 nits, from 100 to well past your HDR peak, sent exactly as coded and told your peak, as films are. Each bar blinks against the top one (white). A bar the TV can't show any brighter looks like white and stops blinking: your HDR peak lies between the last bar that blinks and the first that doesn't.

Find your display's peak: put the TV in its HDR mode with its own dynamic tone mapping off (HGIG, or static). The bars are 400 to 4000 nits side by side, sent untouched. The last bar that still stands apart from the next is your peak. If the TV dims big bright areas, look again with the large window and take the lower answer.

    Updates

    This Cube checks for a new stable version once a day and tells you here; it never installs one by itself. Each update is signed and checked before anything is installed.

    Where this Cube looks for updates (Setup sets it when it installs). A GitHub feed is https://github.com/<owner>/<repo>/releases/download.

    Load by itself

    A profile for each kind of source, loaded when the source changes. Anything changed by hand stays until the next change. The kind playing now is marked. For finer choices (resolution, frame rate, picture shape, the app), use an automation.

    Remote buttons

    The TV button and ☰ held open the on-screen menu. This checks each part, tests the buttons and puts right what it can.

    Remote buttonsAsking the Cube…
    Test
    Start a test, then press the button on the remote. Arrows opens the menu on the screen and checks the remote can move around it.
    Fix
    Switches the key filter back on, lets the menu show over apps and sets this remote’s TV button.

    Automations

    When what plays matches, do something: load a profile, tell Home Assistant (a webhook), switch a device over HDMI-CEC, or send an IR code (lights off as a film starts, on again when it stops). Each runs once when its conditions have held for 2 seconds (or the time you give it), and again only after they stop and start again. An automation's profile wins over Load by itself.
